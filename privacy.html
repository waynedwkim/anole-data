Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 29, 2026

ScaleSpot has no servers and no accounts. We never receive, store, or transmit your personal data.

Location

If you grant location permission, ScaleSpot uses your location to centre the map and to sort nearby results by distance. We never receive it: it stays on your device, except that the map area you are looking at is passed to Apple Maps to run the search itself (see below). ScaleSpot does not log, store, or share your location, and every feature of the app works without granting location access.

Map searches

Shop searches run through Apple Maps (MapKit), so the search text and the map region — which reflects where you are looking, and may be centred on you — are sent to Apple to return results. Those requests are handled by Apple under Apple’s privacy policy; ScaleSpot attaches no identifiers to them and receives nothing back but the search results.

Shop and expo listings

The curated shop list and the expo calendar are refreshed from public files hosted on GitHub, so a shop that closes or a show that moves can be corrected without waiting for an app update. The app requests those files and nothing else — no account, no device identifier, and no information about you is sent with the request. GitHub, like any web host, sees the network request itself under its own privacy statement.

Links out to shops and organizers

When you tap through to a shop’s or an organizer’s website, the link carries utm_source=scalespot. That tells their analytics that a visitor came from ScaleSpot. It is the same for every person who taps it, it says nothing about you, and we never see the result — only the site owner does. We do this so a shop can verify for itself that the app sends real visitors, instead of taking our word for it.

Phone numbers and “Open in Apple Maps” links are never modified.

Calendar

If you use “Add to calendar,” ScaleSpot requests write-only calendar access and creates the single event you chose. It cannot read your existing calendar. Choosing Google Calendar simply opens Google’s event page in your browser.

Show reminders

Reminders are scheduled by iOS on your device. There is no push server, so nothing about which shows you follow ever leaves your phone.

Saved shops

Your saved shops are stored locally on your device and are never uploaded anywhere. Deleting the app deletes them.

Analytics & tracking

The app contains no analytics SDKs, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind.

This website is separate from the app. If it uses analytics at all, it is a cookie-free, aggregate page-count tool that records no personal data and sets no cookies. Nothing on this site is linked to anything in the app.

Children

ScaleSpot is rated 4+ and collects no data from anyone, including children.
