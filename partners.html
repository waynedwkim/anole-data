Your dates, on your site, verified.

We already check your published schedule and keep it in a free app. This page hands that work back to you: a badge for your own site, a calendar your attendees can subscribe to, and a one-click way to tell us the moment something moves.

What this costs you

Nothing, and there is no account to make. The badge is a plain <iframe>. We do not set cookies, we do not track your visitors, and we never ask for exclusivity.

Why we bother verifying

On one pass in August 2026 we found four dates that other listing sites were publishing which the organizer's own page contradicted — one show was postponed with tickets pulled, another was listed from a vendor's schedule that had never been updated. We only take a date from the organizer, or from the venue's own booking calendar.
